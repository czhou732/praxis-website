Undergraduate Curriculum

A rigorous 20-paper sequence designed to take undergraduates from basic neuroscience and programming to reading and writing primary computational psychiatry literature.

Module 1: Foundations of RL and Active Inference

Introduction to Markov Decision Processes, Rescorla-Wagner, and the basics of predictive coding.

  • Sutton & Barto (2018) - Reinforcement Learning: An Introduction (Selected Chapters)
  • Friston (2010) - The free-energy principle: a unified brain theory?

Module 2: Models of Mood and Anhedonia

How does the brain compute value, and what happens when those computations fail?

  • Huys et al. (2013) - Decision-theoretic psychiatry
  • Eldar et al. (2016) - The roles of dopamine in learning and memory

Module 3: Phenotyping & Biomarkers

Moving from subjective clinical interviews to objective computational phenotyping.

  • Montague et al. (2012) - Computational and categorical formalisms in psychiatry
  • Low et al. (2020) - Automated vocal analysis of depression
