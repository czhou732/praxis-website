03A footer you can push around

Fix · Live

The double-well portrait animated too slowly and faintly to notice. Three changes: the pointer becomes a force that perturbs nearby trajectories — you can shove a state out of one well and watch it fall into the other; ring markers at x = ±1 make the two stable states read as destinations; and integration runs fast enough that drift is visible within a couple of seconds.
