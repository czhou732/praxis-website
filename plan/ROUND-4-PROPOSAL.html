01Footer A — strip in its own band

Recommended

The traces get their own 72px band, closed by hairlines above and below, and never share space with text. The instrument line and legal copy sit underneath on clean ground. Move your pointer across the strip — the middle channel's gain rises around the cursor. Reduced motion renders one static frame.

02Footer B — the quiet footer

Alternative

No canvas at all. The instrument line, the disclaimer, and a very faint serif Ψ watermark at the right edge — the mark at 5% ink, like a colophon. The calmest option, and zero JavaScript. If the footer keeps fighting you, this is the honest answer.
