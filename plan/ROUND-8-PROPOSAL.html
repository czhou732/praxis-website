Praxis · Round 8 · Events & Luma, and the mailing list — Visual Proposal

One channel, automated

Two threads. First: the Events page — the record — currently defers everything forward-looking to Speakers. Second: the mailing list is a Google Form that collects addresses someone must manually export, while Luma already automates exactly this: following the calendar emails subscribers when new sessions post. This round folds both into a single low-maintenance channel.

A1Events gets a Luma follow band

Recommended

Events stops being past-only in framing: a second band ("Series on Luma") follows the Sessions record, with two quiet links — follow the calendar, and the .ics download that already exists on Speakers. The header sentence changes from "forward-looking lives under Speakers" to pointing at the band below. No iframe; the embed stays exclusive to the Speakers schedule.
