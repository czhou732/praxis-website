PRAXIS is a research group first and an events organization second. These projects define the intellectual agenda. Members join an existing project or propose their own at the monthly pitch session.

Dopaminergic Voice — crossbenching anhedonia

Active

Extracting acoustic and semantic biomarkers of anhedonia from spontaneous speech using ClinicalWhisper. By crossbenching anhedonia against computational phenotypes, the next step is integrating large language models with reinforcement learning to model reward-prediction errors in vocal flat affect.
