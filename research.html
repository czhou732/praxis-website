Dopaminergic Voice (Crossbenching Anhedonia)

Active Project

Extracting acoustic and semantic biomarkers of anhedonia from spontaneous speech using ClinicalWhisper. By crossbenching anhedonia against computational phenotypes, our next step is to integrate Large Language Models (LLMs) with Reinforcement Learning (RL) to model reward-prediction errors in vocal flat affect.
