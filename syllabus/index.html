Curriculum

A twenty-paper sequence designed to take undergraduates from basic neuroscience and programming to reading — and eventually writing — primary computational psychiatry literature. Nine sessions run across the fall semester.

Module 01

Foundations of RL and active inference

Markov decision processes, Rescorla–Wagner, and the basics of predictive coding.

  • Sutton & Barto (2018) — Reinforcement Learning: An Introduction, selected chapters
  • Friston (2010) — The free-energy principle: a unified brain theory?
Module 02

Models of mood and anhedonia

How the brain computes value, and what happens when those computations fail.

  • Huys et al. (2013) — Decision-theoretic psychiatry
  • Eldar et al. (2016) — The roles of dopamine in learning and memory
Module 03

Phenotyping and biomarkers

Moving from subjective clinical interviews to objective computational phenotyping.

  • Montague et al. (2012) — Computational and categorical formalisms in psychiatry
  • Low et al. (2020) — Automated vocal analysis of depression
